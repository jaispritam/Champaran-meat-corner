About

Champaran Meat Corner focuses on Champaran-style Ahuna handi, with mutton and chicken at the centre of the menu. Alongside the handi you will find roti, ghee paratha, ghee litti, rice and salad, plus special thalis.

You can find us on Jessore Road at Doharia More, Madhyamgram.
